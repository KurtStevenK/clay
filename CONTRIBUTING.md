Keep the existing copyright notices in files you edit. Add your own notice on files you create. Do not remove an upstream author's notice.

Open a pull request against this repository. If something about attribution or licensing worries you, write immediately in an issue. We will cooperate to align with the law and respect everybody's contribution.

Follow the style of the code around your change.
