Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Clay collects no data

Clay has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your images, settings or usage information to the developer or to anyone else.

Your files stay on your Mac

Images you open and save, and Clay's preferences, brushes and other resources, are stored only on your computer. Clay reads files only when you open them.

When Clay uses the network

Clay connects to the internet only when you ask it to, for example to open an image from a web address or to show the online help. Those requests go directly to the site you chose, which has its own privacy policy.

App Store and TestFlight

If you install Clay from the Mac App Store or TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback with the developer when you agree to it in macOS. Apple's privacy policy covers that data.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/KurtStevenK/clay/issues.
